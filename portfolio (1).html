Who I Am

I'm S.M Abdulla Al Emon — a logistics professional who bridges the gap between physical operations and digital systems. Based in Dhaka, I operate at the intersection of supply chain management, ERP implementation, and frontend development.

My journey began on the roads — managing fleets, optimizing routes, coordinating supply chains. Over time I evolved into building the digital backbone of those very operations: ERP workflows, process automation, and dashboards that turn operational chaos into clarity.

I believe great systems — logistical or digital — share the same foundation: clarity of purpose, efficiency of process, and integrity of execution.

Case Studies

Systems I've Built

01

FleetSync — Real-Time Fleet Intelligence Dashboard

🔴 Problem

Large logistics operations rely on disconnected spreadsheets and manual vehicle tracking, causing delays, fuel waste, and zero visibility into live fleet status.

⚡ Solution

Designed a web-based Fleet Intelligence Dashboard integrating VTS data, route analytics, and driver performance metrics into a single real-time interface built with HTML, CSS, JS, and Excel-powered backend reports.

✅ Impact

Concept demonstrates potential to reduce fuel costs by ~25%, cut dispatch response time by 40%, and give operations managers complete live visibility across 100+ vehicles.

VTSJavaScriptExcelFleet Ops
02

LogiFlow — ERP Workflow Automation for Supply Chain

🔴 Problem

Manual purchase orders, vendor approvals, and inventory reconciliation in mid-sized logistics companies create bottlenecks, human errors, and delayed reporting cycles.

⚡ Solution

Conceptualized an ERP module with automated PO generation, multi-level vendor approval workflows, and live inventory sync — designed from real operational pain points experienced in supply chain management.

✅ Impact

Projected to reduce manual processing time by 60%, eliminate approval delays, and provide management with on-demand MIS reports — transforming operations from reactive to proactive.

ERP DesignWorkflow AutomationMISSupply Chain
03

OpsBoard — Operational KPI & Analytics Hub

🔴 Problem

Operations teams often make critical decisions based on outdated or scattered data — weekly reports compiled manually offer no real-time insight into performance trends or emerging risks.

⚡ Solution

Designed a responsive web-based KPI dashboard consolidating logistics performance metrics, cost tracking, delivery success rates, and vendor scorecards — pulling live data via Excel integration and basic API connections.

✅ Impact

Enables daily data-driven decision-making, reduces reporting preparation from hours to minutes, and surfaces hidden inefficiencies before they become costly operational failures.
